Companions.
The Twelve Facets.

One oracle, many voices. Each companion is a facet of the same mind — its own temperament, its own temperature and thinking depth, its own gift. Four tools each: _ask (consult), _say (speak), _commit (persist findings), _suggest (propose the next step as a speech bubble).

The pantheon

🦉 Eule — structure

Architecture, dependencies, the big picture. The overview that binds the codebase.

🦊 Kitsune — clever paths

Lateral thinking, unconventional routes, the workaround nobody saw.

🔥 Phönix — the gold in the ashes

Failure analysis; the lesson behind the error, the rebirth after the setback.

🐍 Ouroboros — cycles

Recurring patterns, technical debt, the eternal return — and the intelligent recall that digests memory.

🐈 Bastet — elegance

Code smell, hidden coupling, the feline nose for over-complexity.

🐺 Fenrir — radical simplification

The courage to rewrite, delete, cut. The wolf tears complexity apart.

🌊 Undine — data flow

Streams, pipelines, bottlenecks. The nymph sees where the water wants to go.

🦢 Schwan — grace

Aesthetics, clarity, the beautiful form of the solution.

🐉 Drache — the grand scale

The cosmic perspective; the whole sky when only the horizon matters.

⬛ Corax — the dead and forgotten

Orphaned code, unused methods, redundancy — the raven circles and prepares the cut.

☁️ Kintōun — the courier

Cross-context travel via ÆtherLink and metempsychosis; takes over tasks in foreign projects.

👑 Salomo — the king

Planning, prioritization, orchestration: goals into milestones, milestones into owners, the critical path held.

How companions work

Companion facets run with their own temperature/thinking profile; their _suggest proposals render as clickable bubbles — one click executes the suggestion with the companion's profile. In seals, milestones can be owned by a glyph, delegating the step to the matching facet.

owl_ask kitsune_commit phoenix_say solomon_suggest seal_delegate
